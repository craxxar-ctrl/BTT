BTT 2.0 DEVELOPABLE RISK GUIDE
==============================

PURPOSE
-------
A Developable Risk describes a progression that can be acquired from world evidence
and then advanced through repeated qualifying events. It is separate from the
Instant-Change risk_tracks embedded in Gender/Heritage/product profiles.

MINIMAL CREATOR CARD
--------------------
Example Risk
ALIASES: example adaptation

ACQUIRE 1: repeated exposure to an enchanted training field
DELIVERY: exposure, repeated-exposure
QUALIFIERS: training field, repeated exposure
ACQUIRE THRESHOLD: 2.25
ACQUIRE HISTORY: 5

TRACK: Adaptive Response
TRIGGERS: repeated training-field exposure, field reacts to the wearer
DELIVERY: exposure, repeated-exposure
TRACK THRESHOLD: 2.2
CHANCE: 10
REPEAT: 10
MAX: 70
MEMORY: 10
SUCCESS 1: HEAD +trigger-sensitive

PERSISTENCE: acquired
REMOVAL: source dispelled, adaptation cleansed

ACQUISITION FIELDS
------------------
ACQUIRE N: <semantic prototype>
  Starts an acquisition path. Multiple ACQUIRE sections are supported.

DELIVERY: <delivery-id, delivery-id>
  Delivery evidence expected for the current acquisition or current track.

QUALIFIERS: <phrase, phrase>
  Additional semantic phrases that strengthen the intended match.

SOURCE TRAITS: trait, trait
SOURCE SLOT TRAITS: trait, trait
SOURCE HERITAGE: heritage-id, heritage-id
SOURCE PROFILE(S): profile-id, profile-id
SOURCE CONDITIONS: condition, condition
  Optional source constraints.

RECEIVER TRAITS: trait, trait
RECEIVER CONDITIONS: condition, condition
  Optional receiver constraints.

EXCLUDE TRAITS: trait, trait
EXCLUDE HERITAGE: heritage-id, heritage-id
EXCLUDE RISKS: risk-id, risk-id
  Optional exclusion gates.

ACQUIRE SLOT: head|arms|legs|breasts|gut|penis|vagina|ass|tail|wings
SLOT ORIGIN: heritage-or-origin-id
NATURAL TRAITS: trait, trait
ACQUIRE AXES: fem +1; height =20
ACQUIRE CONDITIONS: condition, condition
  Optional phenotype/state changes applied when the risk is acquired.

ACQUIRE THRESHOLD: number
ACQUIRE HISTORY: turns
  Confidence threshold and historical corroboration window for acquisition.

TRACK FIELDS
------------
TRACK: <track name>
TRIGGERS: phrase, phrase
DELIVERY / TRACK DELIVERY: delivery-id, delivery-id
QUALIFIERS / TRACK QUALIFIERS: phrase, phrase
SOURCE SLOT TRAITS: trait, trait
RECEIVER ROUTING: slot -> trait + trait; slot -> trait
TRACK THRESHOLD: number
CHANCE: 0-100
REPEAT: 0-100
MAX: 0-100
MEMORY: turns

CHANCE is the starting success chance once an event clears the track evidence
threshold. REPEAT increases the chance after qualifying misses, up to MAX. MEMORY
controls how long track exposure state remains relevant.

SUCCESS GRAMMAR
---------------
A SUCCESS line may contain one or more semicolon-separated outcomes.

Trait outcome:
  SUCCESS 1: HEAD +obedient
  SUCCESS 2: TAIL +prehensile
  SUCCESS 2: BREASTS -sensitive

Axis outcome:
  SUCCESS 1: AXIS fem +1
  SUCCESS 2: AXIS height =20

Condition outcome:
  SUCCESS 1: CONDITION +infection
  SUCCESS 2: CONDITION -infection

Persistent trigger outcome:
  SUCCESS 1: TRIGGER +field-response: reacts automatically to the training field

Multiple outcomes:
  SUCCESS 2: AXIS breast_size +1; AXIS ass_size +1

Mental/behavioral traits are routed to HEAD by BTT's semantic outcome resolver during
AI authoring. AI-generated outcome magnitudes use script-owned balancing defaults
where the engine can resolve the effect itself.

LIFECYCLE
---------
PERSISTENCE: acquired
  The normal generated/creator card default. The risk remains acquired until a
  removal event qualifies.

REMOVAL: phrase, phrase
  Semantic removal events recognized by the runtime.

CREATOR-AUTHORED VS AI-GENERATED
--------------------------------
Creator-authored cards:
- are parsed and executed normally;
- are never automatically rewritten by AI Developable Risk authoring;
- can still show diagnostic Progression Watch evidence.

AI-generated cards contain:
  AI-GENERATED: true

When optional AI generation is enabled, BTT can:
- author a new basic risk after parent recurrence/readiness qualifies;
- replay retained events through the normal acquisition/track runtime;
- watch acquired generated risks for independently corroborated progression;
- append a higher SUCCESS level to an existing track;
- append a genuinely new TRACK when a separate progression family independently
  clears the same recurrence/confidence requirements.

When extending an AI-generated risk, BTT does not re-author its name, acquisitions,
existing tracks, or lifecycle. A new track requests only the new track name, triggers,
outcome semantics, and delivery only when delivery cannot be resolved locally.
No newly authored SUCCESS is applied retroactively.

AI DISCOVERY / AUTHORING MODEL
------------------------------
AI discovery is disabled by default. When enabled, the engine separates:
- canonical event admission;
- affected owner;
- trigger actor;
- causal source/mechanism;
- response channels;
- parent risk hypothesis;
- track hypotheses;
- authoring readiness;
- ordinary post-authoring acquisition/progression.

Weak but real events can count toward recurrence without independently satisfying the
final authoring-confidence threshold. Parent topics remain active without per-event
confidence decay. After the configured inactivity horizon, a parent compacts into one
dormant historical seed at 50% of current authoring confidence and can later be
revived by fresh compatible evidence.
