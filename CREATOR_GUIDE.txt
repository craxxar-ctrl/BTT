BTT 2.0 CREATOR GUIDE
=====================

1. START WITH THE RUNTIME
-------------------------
Install BTT2.0_RC1.txt as one complete Library script. Call BTT("input"),
BTT("context"), and BTT("output") from the corresponding AI Dungeon script tabs.
Do not split or truncate the library.

BTT will create a Configure BTT card and BTT NPC Targets card when story-card access
is available. Settings are read from the Configure BTT Notes/Description field.

2. DEFINE THE SCENARIO'S PROFILE SPACE
--------------------------------------
BTT Gender and BTT Heritage cards define which canonical profiles are available to
that scenario. Include only profiles that are intended to exist in the setting.
Species is represented through Heritage rather than through an independent species
transformation list.

The distribution includes the standard Gender set:
  Male
  Female
  Femboy
  Mascgirl
  Tomboy
  Hermaphrodite
  Futanari
  Dickgirl
  Cuntboy

and the standard Human Heritage profile.

Add scenario-specific Heritage cards as needed. A prose mention alone does not make
an unavailable Heritage profile canonical.

3. ADMIT NPCS DELIBERATELY
--------------------------
BTT tracks the Player automatically.

NPCs can be admitted in either of two ways:
- a Character story card whose title is the character's proper name; or
- the character's proper name listed in the BTT NPC Targets Notes/Description field.

BTT NPC Targets is permission, not proof. The engine still resolves identity/body
and event evidence before creating or changing that NPC's state.

4. CHARACTER-CARD BASELINES
----------------------------
Character cards may include explicit creator metadata:
  baseline_gender = male
  baseline_heritage = human

BTT can also import stable representable physical details from Character cards, such
as hair/eye/skin descriptions, build, cup size, and explicit penis length. Character
card prose is treated as baseline information rather than as a transformation event.

5. UNDERSTAND THE FOUR BTT-MANAGED ENTITY CARDS
-----------------------------------------------
Each initialized Player/NPC owns four cards:

Anatomy BTT
  Canonical identity, per-slot anatomy/traits, active Conditions, overall femininity,
  and relevant debug information.

Appearance BTT
  Structured appearance axes/fields. Player cards may also contain the captured
  AI-authored appearance paragraph. NPC appearance remains structured-only.

Instant-Change BTT
  Temporary/permanent Instant-Change lifecycle, reversion snapshot, Instant-Change
  risk state, Developable Risk state, and machine payload.

AI-Facing BTT
  Concise projection of canonical identity/anatomy/appearance for narrative context.
  NPC AI-Facing cards receive identity aliases as triggers; source/debug cards do not.

Manual edits to managed source cards are imported during the next preflight pass.
BTT then serializes canonical state back through one writer.

6. DEVELOPABLE RISKS
--------------------
Creator-authored Developable Risks are ordinary BTT story cards and are the preferred
way to define important planned progression systems. See DEVELOPABLE_RISK_GUIDE.txt.

The optional AI-authoring subsystem can discover non-standard repeated-exposure risks
that were not pre-authored. It is disabled by default. When enabled, it:
- collects actual owner-bound response/change events;
- resolves parent source/mechanism hypotheses with confidence scoring;
- waits for recurrence and authoring-readiness confidence;
- asks the AI only for unresolved semantic wording;
- compiles creator-card grammar and balancing values itself;
- replays retained evidence through the normal Developable Risk runtime;
- keeps acquired generated risks under Progression Watch;
- may add higher SUCCESS levels or independently qualified new tracks to AI-generated
  cards only.

Creator-authored Developable Risk cards remain immutable to this subsystem.

7. INSTANT-CHANGE PROFILES
--------------------------
Gender/Heritage profiles may also contain Instant-Change detection, duration,
duration modifiers, permanence/reversion rules, and Instant-Change risk_tracks.
These Instant-Change risk_tracks are a different subsystem from BTT Developable Risk
cards; do not mix the two grammars.

8. APPEARANCE REFRESH
---------------------
Player appearance prose can be refreshed through a tagged metadata operation when
appearance_refresh_enabled is true and the configured refresh conditions are met.
The task asks the model for:
  (player_appearance = `<1-4 concise appearance sentences>`)

BTT strips the metadata operation from visible story output and stores the prose.
NPCs never request AI appearance refreshes.

9. RELEASE-SAFE CREATOR WORKFLOW
--------------------------------
Recommended order for a scenario release:
1. Install/freeze the BTT runtime version.
2. Add the standard Gender profiles.
3. Add only scenario-valid Heritage profiles.
4. Set Character-card baseline metadata where useful.
5. Add creator-authored Developable Risks that are part of intended scenario design.
6. Configure BTT settings.
7. Add explicit NPC targets only where needed.
8. Run a fresh scenario test and inspect the four BTT cards plus debug output.
9. Enable AI Developable Risk generation only if the scenario benefits from emergent
   non-standard progression systems.
